# UX PROJECT SCENARIOS

## SCENARIO 1 – HEALTH AND FITNESS APP

### 1. Scenario Description

A Health and Fitness App helps users track their daily workouts, health statistics, meals, sleep, and fitness progress. The application is designed to help users maintain a healthy lifestyle and achieve their fitness goals.

### 2. User Pain Points

* Users find it difficult to track different health activities in one place.
* Users may forget to record their daily workouts.
* Fitness progress can be difficult to understand.
* Beginners may find the application complicated.
* Users may lose motivation when they do not see quick results.
### 3. Problem Statements

**Problem Statement 1:**

Health-conscious users need a way to track their daily fitness activities because managing exercise, food, water, and sleep separately is difficult, which results in incomplete tracking and reduced motivation.

**Problem Statement 2:**

Beginner fitness users need a way to understand their fitness progress because health data can be confusing, which results in difficulty maintaining consistent healthy habits.

### 4. Appropriate Research Methods

#### User Interviews

User interviews can be conducted with people who regularly use fitness applications.
**Justification:**
Interviews help understand users' personal experiences, needs, expectations, and difficulties while using fitness applications.

#### Usability Testing

Users can be asked to perform tasks such as recording a workout, checking their progress, and setting a fitness goal.

**Justification:**
Usability testing helps identify problems with navigation, buttons, layout, and the overall ease of using the application.

#### Surveys

Surveys can be conducted among a larger group of fitness app users.

**Justification:**
Surveys help collect information from many users and identify common problems and preferences.

# SCENARIO 2 – GOVERNMENT SERVICE PORTAL

### 1. Scenario Description

A Government Service Portal allows citizens to apply for certificates, access government services, submit applications, and check application status online. The portal aims to reduce the need for physical visits to government offices.

### 2. User Pain Points

* Users have difficulty finding the required service.
* Government terminology can be difficult to understand.
* Users may not know which documents are required.
* Application status may be difficult to find.
* Users may have difficulty completing online forms.
* Older users may struggle with complex navigation.

### 3. Problem Statements

**Problem Statement 1:**

Citizens need a way to find and apply for government services because the portal has complex navigation and unfamiliar terminology, which results in confusion and incomplete applications.

**Problem Statement 2:**

Citizens need a way to track their application status because status information is difficult to locate or understand, which results in frustration and repeated enquiries.

### 4. Appropriate Research Methods

#### User Interviews

Interviews can be conducted with citizens who have used government service portals.

**Justification:**
Interviews help understand users' difficulties with forms, terminology, document requirements, and navigation.

#### Usability Testing

Users can be asked to find a service, submit an application, upload documents, and check application status.

**Justification:**
Usability testing helps identify actual problems users face while completing important tasks.
